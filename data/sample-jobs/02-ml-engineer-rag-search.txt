Machine Learning Engineer, Retrieval and RAG (Remote, India)
Lumen Search Labs (a fictional company, written for FactFit demos)

About us
Lumen builds question-answering over private document collections for small legal and finance teams. Our product lives or dies on retrieval quality, so we measure everything.

The role
You will own the retrieval stack end to end: indexing, hybrid search, reranking and evaluation of generated answers.

Responsibilities
- Design and tune hybrid retrieval that combines keyword search (BM25) with dense embeddings.
- Build evaluation harnesses for retrieval (nDCG, recall) and for generated answers (faithfulness, citation quality).
- Decide, with data, whether a reranker or a bigger model is worth its latency.
- Run vector search at scale; HNSW and approximate nearest neighbour experience is a big plus.
- Ship Python services and demos; Streamlit or FastAPI is fine.
- Write up experiments clearly, with confidence intervals where it matters.

Requirements
- Strong Python; PyTorch and Hugging Face or Sentence-Transformers experience.
- Hands-on experience building a RAG system and measuring it.
- Good statistics sense: you know why a test split must stay untouched.
- Experience with LangChain or LangGraph is nice to have.
- Experience with Azure OpenAI or Databricks is nice to have.
- Published research or a paper is a plus.

Compensation and logistics are discussed after a first conversation.
